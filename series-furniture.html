Woodworking & Craft

Handcrafted Furniture

Beyond painting and sculpture, Sem is a skilled woodworker who designs and builds functional furniture — chairs, tables, shelves, and benches crafted with traditional joinery and sculptural sensibility.

The Furniture collection represents another dimension of Loh Ek Sem's artistic practice — functional objects crafted from wood with the same attention to form and detail that characterizes his fine art. Each piece is designed and built by hand in his Johor Bahru workshop.

Sem's furniture combines traditional woodworking techniques with sculptural vision. A bookshelf becomes an architectural element. A bench is designed for both seating and display. A writing table references Chippendale elegance while serving contemporary function. The pieces are built to last — solid wood construction, careful joinery, finishes that honor the material.

The collection includes custom commissions and personal projects — pieces made for family, friends, and his own home and garden. Many works blur the line between furniture and sculpture, reflecting Sem's belief that functional objects can also be art.
