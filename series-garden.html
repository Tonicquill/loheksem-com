Landscape & Water Features

Garden

Hand-built fiberglass lotus pond, water features, and garden sculptures — a personal sanctuary where art meets nature in the artist's own backyard.

The Garden collection documents Loh Ek Sem's hand-built garden sanctuary at his Johor Bahru home — featuring a custom fiberglass lotus pond, sculptural installations, and landscaped patios created between 2000-2010.

Unlike traditional garden documentation, these images reveal the garden as a living artwork. The centerpiece is Sem's hand-built fiberglass lotus pond — constructed by hand with curved edges, integrated planters, and a stone bridge. Around it, garden rooms unfold: front patios with ceramic displays, side porches with water features, sculpture corners, and the famous "turtle tunnel" archway.

The garden served multiple purposes — personal sanctuary, outdoor gallery for sculptures, and testing ground for water feature designs that would later appear in client commissions. Evening shots reveal the garden transformed by lighting, with lotus blooms glowing against dark water.

Featured: The Lotus Pond

Deck Bench & Lotus Pond

The centerpiece — hand-built fiberglass pond with integrated deck bench. Night photography captures the reflective water surface and blooming lotus flowers.

Stone Bridge

Custom stone bridge spanning the pond, connecting garden zones. The bridge became a gathering spot and viewing platform for lotus blooms.

Artist's Statement

"The garden was my personal laboratory. I built the pond myself — from designing the fiberglass shell to planting the first lotus tubers. It took months of trial and error to get the water circulation right, to prevent algae, to create an ecosystem where lotus could thrive. The garden became an extension of my studio — sculptures found natural settings among the plants, and the changing seasons provided endless subjects."
