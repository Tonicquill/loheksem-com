## Part 2: Platform-by-Platform Strategy

### Website (Primary Hub)

**Technical Standards (2025-2026):**
- Image formats: WebP/AVIF (80% smaller than JPEG)
- Load time: <3s on 4G (Core Web Vitals threshold)
- Mobile-first: 60%+ traffic is mobile
- SEO: Keyword-rich titles, alt text, schema markup

**Content Structure:**
- Homepage: Orientation + highlights (3-second value prop)
- Series pages: Dedicated context per body of work
- About: Artist narrative + portrait
- Exhibitions: Verified timeline + press
- Contact: Form + email + location

**Design Patterns:**
- Asymmetric layouts (DESIGN_VARIANCE=8)
- Non-center bias for hero sections
- Masonry grids for galleries
- Staggered animations (100ms delay cascade)

---

### Instagram (Visual Discovery)

**2025-2026 Algorithm Changes:**
- **SEO > Hashtags**: Captions with keywords outrank hashtag-heavy posts
- **Engagement velocity**: First 60 minutes critical for reach
- **Format priority**: Reels > Carousels > Single images
- **Aspect ratio**: 4:5 portrait (1080x1350) occupies more screen space

**Content Strategy:**
- **Reels**: Process videos (paper cast making), studio tours, exhibition walkthroughs (15-30s)
- **Carousels**: Before/after, series deep-dives, cultural context slides
- **Stories**: Behind-the-scenes, Q&A, exhibition prep (24h ephemeral)
- **Highlights**: Permanent archives (Process, Exhibitions, Press, About)

**Posting Cadence:**
- 4-5 posts/week (mix of Reels + Carousels)
- Stories daily during exhibition periods
- Consistent posting time (algorithm rewards predictability)

**SEO Implementation:**
- Bio: "Loh Ek Sem | Malaysian Artist | Kampung Life Documentarian | Johor Bahru"
- Captions: Natural keyword integration ("kampung house," "Peranakan culture," "paper casting technique")
- Alt text: Descriptive image context (not just "painting")

---

### Pinterest (Evergreen Traffic)

**Why Pinterest for Artists:**
- Pins have 3-4 month half-life (vs. 24h Instagram)
- Drives referral traffic to website (clickable links)
- Users actively search for art/architecture/decor inspiration
- No hashtag dependency — pure SEO play

**Pin Strategy:**
- **Aspect ratio**: 2:3 vertical (1000x1500px optimal)
- **Content types**:
  - Artwork close-ups with title/dimensions overlay
  - Process step graphics (paper cast making)
  - Room mockups (artwork in interior context)
  - Cultural context cards (kampung life explainer)
- **Keywords**: Title + description + board names (no hashtags needed)

**Board Structure:**
1. Malay Kampung Series
2. Peranakan Heritage Art
3. Paper Cast Sculptures
4. Watercolour Studies
5. Malaysian Artist Archive
6. Kampung House Architecture (cultural context)
7. Art Process & Technique

**Posting Cadence:**
- 5-10 pins/week (mix of new + repinned content)
- Tailwind scheduler for consistency
- Rich Pins enabled (auto-pulls metadata from website)

**Timeline:**
- Month 1: Setup + initial boards
- Month 2-3: Consistent pinning, ranking begins
- Month 4+: Evergreen traffic compounds

---

### X/Twitter (Industry Networking)

**2025-2026 Reality:**
- 60-70% organic reach drop since 2023 algorithm changes
- Still valuable for: curator connections, journalist relationships, artist community
- Thread format ideal for long-form storytelling

**Content Strategy:**
- **Threads**: Oral history excerpts, exhibition stories, technique explainers
- **Engagement**: Reply to art journalists, curators, museum accounts
- **Timing**: Post during US/EU waking hours for global reach

**Posting Cadence:**
- 3-5 tweets/week (quality over quantity)
- 1 thread/month (deep-dive content)
- Engage with 10-15 industry accounts/week

**Thread Topics for Sem:**
- "How I documented vanishing kampung life for 30 years"
- "The 7-step paper casting technique I invented"
- "What Peranakan culture taught me about art"

## Part 5: Technical Implementation Notes

### Image Optimization Pipeline
```
Original (E: drive) → Resize (max 2000px) → Convert WebP → Compress (80%) → Upload
Hero images: <500KB
Gallery images: <300KB
Thumbnails: <50KB
```

### Lazy Loading Strategy
```html
<img src="hero.webp" alt="..." loading="eager">  <!-- Above fold -->
<img src="gallery-1.webp" alt="..." loading="lazy">  <!-- Below fold -->
```
