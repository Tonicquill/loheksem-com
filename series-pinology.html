Installation Art & Record Holder

Cultural Pin Carnival

A monumental pin art installation depicting Peranakan culture using 3.7 million map pins — recognized by the Malaysian Book of Records. Created for JCI Foon Yew at Persada Johor in 2009.

Malaysian Book of Records

"Pinology" — Cultural Pin Carnival holds the record for the largest pin art installation in Malaysia. The artwork measures 9.35 meters by 6.7 meters and consists of 3.7 million map pins meticulously placed to form figures in traditional Peranakan attire.

The Cultural Pin Carnival represents one of the most ambitious installation art projects in Johor — a massive pin art mural created for the JCI Foon Yew charity fundraiser. The work depicts figures in traditional Nyonya and Baba attire, celebrating the Peranakan heritage of Malaysia.

Each pin was placed by hand — a labor-intensive process that required hundreds of hours of meticulous work. The resulting image emerges from thousands of colored dots, similar to pointillism but executed with map pins instead of paint. The technique creates a unique texture and depth that changes when viewed from different distances.

The installation was displayed at Persada Johor and documented by New Straits Times in June 2009, which reported on the record-breaking achievement and the charitable purpose behind the project.

Press Coverage

Largest Cultural Pin Art in the Country

New Straits Times — June 12, 2009 | By Chuah Bee Kim

This article details the creation of "Peranakan," a 9.35m × 6.7m artwork made of 3.7 million map pins. The project was inspired by Loh Ek Sem and created to raise funds for six charities. The installation features figures in traditional Peranakan attire, showcasing the hybrid Chinese-Malay culture that defines the Nyonya heritage.
