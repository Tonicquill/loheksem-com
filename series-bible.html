Scripture & Faith

Bible Series

Biblical narratives and scripture scenes rendered with documentary detail — exploring faith, spirituality, and the timeless stories that have shaped moral consciousness across centuries.

The Bible Series represents Loh Ek Sem's engagement with scripture and religious narrative. These paintings depict key biblical scenes and characters, rendered with the same attention to cultural and historical detail that characterizes his work across all series.

Working primarily in oil and acrylic on canvas, Sem brings a Southeast Asian perspective to these universal stories. The series includes depictions of New Testament narratives, Old Testament scenes, and symbolic religious imagery.
